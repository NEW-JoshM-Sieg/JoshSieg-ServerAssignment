# Roast Profile - Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Profile used for the Yirgacheffe in the **Holiday Blend** (profile B). Logged with the roast-profile-logger tool.

## Bean
- Ethiopia Yirgacheffe, Grade 1, washed (Highland Imports, Tom Becker)
- Density: high, moisture 10.5%

## Tasting notes
- Blueberry, jasmine, lemon zest
- In the blend, the Colombia Huila adds caramel and body

## Tips
- Lower gas slightly after first crack so development doesn't run past 2:30
- Rest 3 days before cupping
