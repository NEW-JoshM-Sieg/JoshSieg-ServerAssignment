# Brand Voice Guide

How Riverstone Coffee Roasters sounds everywhere: website, labels, emails, social media and the market booth.

## Who we are
A small roaster in the Hudson Valley. We know the people who grow and import our coffee, and we roast in small batches.

## Voice
- **Warm**: talk like a friendly barista, not a corporation
- **Plain**: explain coffee terms the first time ("washed means the fruit is removed before drying")
- **Specific**: name the origin, the importer, the roast
- **Not snobby**: there's no wrong way to enjoy coffee

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Bright berry notes from Ethiopia" | "An exquisite symphony of terroir" |
| "Roasted Tuesday, shipped Wednesday" | "Artisanally curated freshness" |
| "Ask us anything" | "Per our policy" |
