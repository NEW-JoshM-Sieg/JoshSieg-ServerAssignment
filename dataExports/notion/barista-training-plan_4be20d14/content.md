# Barista Training Plan

Two-week onboarding for new baristas. Owned by Dana Whitfield. The checklist and quiz live in the barista-training GitHub repo.

## Week 1: Foundations
- Opening and closing
- Espresso: 18 g in, 36 g out, 27-32 s
- Milk steaming and drinks menu
- Point of sale

## Week 2: Coffee knowledge
- Pour-over at 1:16
- Our coffees: house espresso, single origins, **Holiday Blend** (Yirgacheffe + Huila, profile B)
- Our suppliers: Highland Imports (Tom Becker)
- Wholesale pickups (Corner Table Cafe and others)

## Sign-off
- [ ] Shadow 3 shifts
- [ ] Pass the quiz with 8/10 or better
- [ ] Final check with Dana Whitfield
